Discovery will find more than you can review in a week. This page is the order to work in, what to look at for each
asset, and the decisions OpenController lets you take. It assumes a read credential on every platform and a control
credential where you intend to act.

## Triage: what to look at first

Work from the findings, not the list. Findings already rank the inventory by what an asset can do and how exposed it
is, and a finding's severity is raised when the asset is reachable from outside, acts through tools, reads private
data or is in use.

| Look first at | Why |
|---|---|
| **Critical and High findings on shadow agents** | Reachable, acting, or reading data, with no owner. These are the incidents waiting to happen. |
| **Agents shared organization-wide or published to a catalog** | The widest audience for whatever data sits behind them. |
| **Agents acting with their maker's credentials** | A person's permissions, running unattended. |
| **Identities and keys with no agent** | Keys that never expire or have not been used in 30 days belong to something that is gone or forgotten. |
| **External MCP servers** | Code from outside the organization, running on an agent's behalf. |
| **Everything else that is shadow** | Register in bulk by connection once the above is handled, and let approvals do the sorting. |

## Investigate: the questions for each asset

Open the asset in **AI Inventory**. Its page answers most of these.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who owns it, and is it still needed?">
    Discovery records the person the platform names as creator or owner, and prefills them when you register. If
    that person has left, the asset has no owner. Ask the team whether the agent is in use; the asset's status and,
    with observability, its traces tell you whether it runs at all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What identity does it run as?">
    The identity relationship shows the service principal, role or key. Check whether it is the maker's own
    credentials, whether it is shared with other assets, and what that identity can reach.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can it reach?">
    Models, tools, MCP servers, knowledge bases and memories are listed as relationships. An agent that reads a
    customer table through a tool is a data access path, and should be reviewed as one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can use it?">
    The Access card shows the people and groups it is shared with, and whether sharing is organization-wide or
    public. On platforms that support it, the same card is where you change that.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which findings does it have?">
    The asset's findings list the policies it fails and the factors that raised them. The frameworks page says
    which control each policy maps to, for the record.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Decide: the options and when to use them

| Decision | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Register** | The agent is needed, has an owner, and its reach is acceptable | Request registration from Shadow AI or the asset; an approver registers it in Approvals |
| **Reject** | It should not exist as it is; the requester may fix and resubmit | Reject in Approvals, with a reason |
| **Narrow the audience** | The agent is fine, the sharing is not | On the Access card: end organization-wide sharing, or remove people and groups |
| **Block or quarantine** | Stop it now, reversibly, while you investigate | Block on the asset; the dialog can register it as pending in the same step. Unblock when done |
| **Route through the gateway** | The agent should keep running, under guardrails and a budget | Point its model calls at the LLM gateway with a virtual key |
| **Delete** | It is abandoned, or must not exist | Delete on the asset; the name must be typed, and it cannot be undone |
| **Revoke** | A registered agent no longer qualifies | Revoke in the Registry; the asset returns to shadow |

Which of these a platform supports differs, and the control page lists every combination.

## After the decision

- **Rescan.** After a change, the asset's cloud is rescanned and the asset's card shows the change as pending until
  the scan confirms it.
- **Audit.** Every action, approval and rejection is in the audit log with who did it and when.
- **Keep the gate closed.** Set the registration policy so the kinds that matter must be registered, and let the
  Shadow AI count tell you when something new appears. Findings re-run on every scan.
