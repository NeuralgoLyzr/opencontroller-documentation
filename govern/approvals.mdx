Approving a request registers the asset: it moves from **Shadow AI** to the **Registry**, with its owner and purpose on record. Rejecting it leaves the asset shadow.

<Note>
Before you start:
- Your deployment runs the registry (the Register and control block). If not, the page says **Approvals is not part of this organization's opencontroller**.
- Deciding needs the **Admin** role (`registry:decide`). Anyone with **Viewer** can read the queue.
- Someone has requested a registration, from [Shadow AI](/discover/shadow-ai), the asset's details, or [Register external agent](/govern/registry#register-an-external-agent).
</Note>

## Decide a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Approvals">
    Go to **Govern** > **Approvals**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the request">
    The **Pending** tab lists each request with its **Asset**, **Kind**, **Owner**, and who requested it and when. Type in **Search** to find a request by name, owner or purpose.

    {/* TODO verify: the Pending table header reads Asset, Kind, Owner, Checks, Requested, but the requester and time show under Checks and the Requested column is empty. Check the console before capturing approvals-list. */}

    {/* SCREENSHOT: approvals-list | crop | Approvals on the Pending tab. Owners and requesters blurred. */}
    <Frame caption="Approvals, Pending">
      <img src="/images/govern/approvals-list.png" alt="The Approvals page on the Pending tab, with the requests waiting and the Approve selected and Reject selected buttons." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the request">
    Select the row. A side panel shows the **Owner** (marked **from the platform** when the platform names them), the **Purpose**, who **Requested** it and when, the **Policy** version, and the **History**. Select the link named after its kind (for example, **Agent in the inventory**) to open the asset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    - Select **Approve**. In **Approve registration**, add a **Note** if you want, then select **Approve**.
    - Or select **Reject**. In **Reject registration**, type **Why is it rejected?** (required), then select **Reject**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>If **Separation of duties** is on in [Policies](/govern/policies), you cannot approve a request you made yourself. **Approve** is greyed out and says **You requested it: another administrator approves it**. It is on by default.</Warning>

## Withdraw a request

The person who requested a registration, or an Admin, can withdraw it while it waits. Open the request and select **Withdraw**, then **Withdraw** again in **Withdraw request**. The asset stays shadow.

## Check it worked

- A message says *asset name* **registered**, or *asset name* **rejected; it stays shadow**.
- An approved asset is listed on [Registry](/govern/registry), and its **Registration** card says **Registered**.
- A rejected, revoked or withdrawn request moves to the **Decided** tab. The **Outcome** column shows **Rejected**, **Revoked** or **Withdrawn**. Open it to read who decided and why.
