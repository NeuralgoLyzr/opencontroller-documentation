A monitor judges on a schedule and compares each run with the ones before, so it can say an agent got worse. A dataset is a test set you grade by hand, and an experiment answers and grades every row to tell you whether a change helped. Prompts keep the prompts you try in versions. Connections are where all of it reads and judges.

## Monitors

The **Monitors** table lists each monitor with its **Checks**, **Schedule**, **Last run**, **Next run**, and a **Run** button. A monitor's page says whether it is **Scheduled** or **Paused**, with **Edit**, **Pause** or **Resume**, and **Run now**; it shows its **Runs**, when it runs **Next**, what it **Watches**, what it **Measures** and where it **Alerts**, a **Trend** over its runs, and each run's report.

A run's report lists its findings as critical, warning or info; a **Detectors** table with each measure **Now**, **Before**, the **Change**, its p value and the **Traces** behind it; failures by step; loops; and for each check its metrics, labels and the worst records judged.

### The editor

| Section | Settings |
|---|---|
| **What it watches** | **Name**, **Description**, the **Trace store** and a **Project or service** in it |
| **Detectors** | Built-in measures that need no model: **Errors** (traces that fail, and the failures grouped by step and message, new ones called out), **Latency** (the p95), **Token bloat** (the tokens a trace spends), **Cost** (where spans record it) and **Loops and retries** (one call made again and again, or a failing one retried) |
| **Checks** | **Add a check** picks an evaluator; each has **Only records where** (a filter), its **Variables** mapped to fields, and **Thresholds** |
| **Schedule** | **Hourly**, **Every 6 hours**, **Daily**, **Weekdays** or **Weekly** at a time, or a **Cron** expression, in a **Time zone** |
| **Judging** | **Records per check** (10 to 1000), **Look back hours** (1 to 720), **Baseline runs** (1 to 30 earlier runs to compare with) and **Tolerance** (0 to 1) |
| **Alerts** | A **Webhook** URL, **Send a test alert**, and **Alert at**: **Critical only**, **Warnings and worse** or **Every finding**. Slack and Teams incoming webhooks are recognized and formatted for. |

A change is reported when the mean moved by at least the tolerance and the difference is significant at the 5% level, with each mean on at least 10 scores.

## Datasets and experiments

The **Datasets** table lists each with its **Rows**, **Latest experiment** and when it **Changed**. **New dataset** asks for a **Name** and **Description**. A dataset's page has **Rows** and **Experiments** views.

**Add rows** offers three ways:

- **Write a row** by hand.
- **Import a CSV or JSON file**.
- **Capture from traces**: a **Trace store**, a **Project**, whether each item is a span, trace or session and which step, **Only where** (a filter), **From** the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, how many **Records** (1 to 1000), **One item per question** to split a conversation into turns, and **Seed expected answers** to fill the expected column with what the agent said, for you to correct.

**Run experiment** then asks:

<Steps>
<Step title="Where do the answers come from">
**Saved answers** already in the rows, **A prompt** from the hub run over each row, or **Your agent's latest answers** fetched from its traces.
</Step>
<Step title="Who grades them">
One or more evaluators, each with its variables mapped to the dataset's columns, and **Your labels column**, the grades you gave, so the experiment can measure how often the judge agrees with you.
</Step>
<Step title="Name it">
A name, **Compare with earlier run** to see rows that got worse or better, and **Advanced** pass rules that say when the experiment counts as held.
</Step>
</Steps>

The experiments table shows each run's **Answers**, **Scores**, whether its **Pass rule** was **Held** or **Not held**, and when it ran. An experiment's page has a scorecard per evaluator with its agreement with your labels and the rows that got worse or better, and a rows filter: **All**, **Disagrees with you**, **Worse**, **Better** or **Failed**.

## Prompts

A prompt is a list of messages with `{variables}`, a **Model connection**, **Model**, **Temperature** and the **Longest answer in tokens**. Saving makes a version, with a note. **Try it** runs the prompt as written, saved or not, on **A few rows** of a dataset or on **Words typed in**; nothing is kept. A saved prompt can be run on a whole dataset as an experiment.

## Connections

Trace stores are where records are read from: **ClickHouse**, or Arize. Models judge them: Anthropic's API, or any OpenAI-compatible one, such as the [LLM gateway](/runtime/llm-gateway). Secrets are sealed in the evals service and never shown again.

**Trace store** and **Model** open the same dialog: a **Kind**, a **Name**, an **Address** (for a model, the API's address up to `/v1`; for a store, its HTTP interface on port 8123), a **User** for ClickHouse, an **API key** or **Password**, and the store's options: **Database** and **Traces table** for ClickHouse, or a **Space ID** for Arize. Saving checks the connection; the table shows each one's Name, Type, Address and **Check** (**Works**, or the error). **Edit** and **Remove** are on each row; a connection an evaluator, task or monitor uses cannot be removed.

<Note>
The evals service reads the trace store with its own connection, not the one on Settings, so give it the same read-only ClickHouse user you gave [Observability](/monitor/observability).
</Note>
