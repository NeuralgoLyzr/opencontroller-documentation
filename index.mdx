Your organisation already runs more AI agents than anyone has counted. They were built in Copilot Studio, Bedrock,
Vertex AI, Databricks and Snowflake by teams who needed them, and most of them never passed a security review. Each
one holds an identity, reaches data, calls tools and talks to people. Nobody owns the list.

OpenController is the control plane that owns the list. It connects to each cloud and platform with read access,
builds one inventory of every agent and everything each agent uses, and gives you the authority to
decide which agents are allowed, stop the ones that are not, and watch the ones that run. Agents stay where they
were built. Nothing moves.

## Who it is for

- **Security** teams accountable for AI they did not build and cannot see. OpenController gives them the
  inventory, the owner of every agent, the findings, and the stop button.
- **AI and platform leaders** who want teams to build agents without a review bottleneck. The registry and the
  gateway make the approved path the easy path.
- **Governance, risk and compliance** who need evidence: which AI systems exist, who owns them, what controls apply,
  and how that maps to the frameworks auditors ask about.

## How it treats your environment

- **Read-only by default.** Discovery lists and describes. It never invokes an agent, reads a conversation or
  fetches a secret value.
- **Control is opt-in, per platform.** Blocking, sharing and deleting need a second credential you grant when you
  are ready, and every action is audited.
- **Deployed in your environment.** One installation per organisation, with secrets in HashiCorp Vault. Traces and
  logs stay in your trace store.
