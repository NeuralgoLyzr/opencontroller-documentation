<div className="oc-hero">

<p>Your organization already runs more AI agents than anyone has counted. Nobody owns the list.</p>

<p>They were built in Copilot Studio, Bedrock, Vertex AI, Databricks and Snowflake by teams who needed them, and most of them never passed a security review. Each one holds an identity, reaches data, calls tools and talks to people.</p>

<p>OpenController is the control plane that owns the list. It connects to each cloud and platform with read access, builds one inventory of every agent and everything each agent uses, and gives you the authority to decide which agents are allowed, stop the ones that are not, and watch the ones that run. Agents stay where they were built. Nothing moves.</p>

## One platform, six capabilities

<div className="oc-ledger">
  <div className="oc-row">
    <Icon icon="radar" />
    <a href="/discover/inventory">AI discovery</a>
    <p><strong>One inventory</strong> of every agent, model, MCP server, tool, identity and memory across AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, Databricks, Anthropic and Snowflake, read on a schedule. The Shadow AI view shows what nobody has registered.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oc-row">
    <Icon icon="clipboard-check" />
    <a href="/govern/registry">Governance</a>
    <p><strong>A registry</strong> of approved agents with named owners, a registration policy that says what must be registered, approvals with separation of duties, and an audit trail.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oc-row">
    <Icon icon="octagon-x" />
    <a href="/govern/control-actions">Control</a>
    <p><strong>Block or quarantine</strong> an agent, end organization-wide sharing, remove a person's access, or delete it, on the platform it runs on, with a control credential you grant per platform.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oc-row">
    <Icon icon="shield" />
    <a href="/runtime/llm-gateway">Runtime security</a>
    <p><strong>An LLM gateway</strong> in front of every model provider, with virtual keys, budgets and logs, and guardrails that block, redact or mask personal data, secrets, prompt injection and off-topic use in prompts and answers.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oc-row">
    <Icon icon="scan-search" />
    <a href="/posture/findings">Posture</a>
    <p><strong>Thirty security policies</strong> checked continuously against the inventory, raising findings with severities, mapped to the OWASP LLM and agentic Top 10, MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001 and the EU AI Act.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="oc-row">
    <Icon icon="activity" />
    <a href="/monitor/observability">Observability and evals</a>
    <p><strong>Traces</strong> of what agents did and what they cost, issues surfaced from them, and evaluators that score agents over time so you know when one gets worse.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Who it is for

<div className="oc-cols">
  <div>
    <div className="oc-t">Security</div>
    <p>Teams accountable for AI they did not build and cannot see. OpenController gives them the inventory, the owner of every agent, the findings, and the stop button.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <div className="oc-t">AI and platform leaders</div>
    <p>Those who want teams to build agents without a review bottleneck. The registry and the gateway make the approved path the easy path.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <div className="oc-t">Governance, risk and compliance</div>
    <p>Those who need evidence: which AI systems exist, who owns them, what controls apply, and how that maps to the frameworks auditors ask about.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## How it treats your environment

<div className="oc-strip">
  <div>
    <div className="oc-t">Read-only by default</div>
    <p>Discovery lists and describes. It never invokes an agent, reads a conversation or fetches a secret value.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <div className="oc-t">Control is opt-in, per platform</div>
    <p>Blocking, sharing and deleting need a second credential you grant when you are ready, and every action is audited.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <div className="oc-t">Deployed in your environment</div>
    <p>One installation per organization, with secrets in HashiCorp Vault. Traces and logs stay in your trace store.</p>
  </div>
</div>
