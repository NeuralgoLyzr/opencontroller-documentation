---
title: "What is OpenController"
description: "One inventory of every AI agent across your clouds, with the controls to govern, run and watch them."
---

OpenController is a control plane for the AI agents an organisation builds and buys. It connects to the clouds and
platforms where agents live, reads what is there, and gives security and platform teams one place to see every agent,
decide which ones are allowed, act on the ones that are not, and watch the ones that run.

It does this without moving anything. Agents stay where they were built. OpenController holds read access to each
platform, and, where you grant it, the narrower access it needs to act.

## Who it is for

- **Security teams** who need to know what AI is running, who owns it, what it can reach, and how to stop it.
- **Platform teams** who run the gateway, the guardrails and the observability the agents depend on.
- **Governance and risk** who need the registry, the approvals and the mapping to frameworks such as the OWASP LLM
  Top 10, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001 and the EU AI Act.

## Where it runs

OpenController is deployed in your own environment, one installation per organisation. Secrets live in HashiCorp
Vault. Nothing about your agents leaves your environment unless you
connect a module that calls out, such as the assistant.
