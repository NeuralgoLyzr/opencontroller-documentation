The map is the estate as a graph: every account's agents and what they use, across the clouds. Its default coloring is compliance, so an agent calling a shadow MCP server shows a red edge into it. The same map serves two pages.

**Where it lives:** the map is the **Map** view of both AI Inventory and Shadow AI. On AI Inventory it opens on the whole estate; on Shadow AI it opens with **Non-compliant only** on. The old `/graph` and `/risk-map` addresses redirect to the Shadow AI map. There is no separate graph page.

## How to read the Layers layout

The default layout, **Layers**, draws the estate left to right in four columns, each meaning one thing:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Accounts** | Each connected account, named after its connection, with its cloud |
| **Products** | The products agents were built in, for example Copilot Studio or Bedrock AgentCore, folded with a count |
| **Agents** | The agents of each open product |
| **What they use** | The assets the agents reach, folded by kind with a count |

Products and kinds start folded. Opening a product shows its agents; opening a kind shows its assets. Links join only neighbouring columns, so none crosses the map. When more agents or assets exist than fit, a **+N more** tile appears; the rest are in the list view. The first time the map loads, the account with the most agents is opened.

Picking an agent or asset traces its paths and dims the rest. Only what is not compliant is colored, so risk stands out. Hovering a folded tile says how many of its assets are not compliant.

## The toolbar

- **Find agents, models, tools…**: filters the map to what matches.
- A cloud selector, shown when more than one cloud is present: **All clouds** or one.
- **Non-compliant only**: keeps only assets that are shadow or awaiting approval, and every asset with a path into one. The count beside it is the number of non-compliant assets. When it is on and nothing is non-compliant, the map says so and tells you to turn it off to see the whole estate.
- **View** opens a menu:
  - **Show**: **The whole estate**, or **One asset and what it touches** (the focus view).
  - **Colour by**: **Compliance** (red Shadow, amber Awaiting approval, green Registered, grey Not governed) or **Kind**.
  - **Layout**: **Layers**, **Neat** or **Graph**. Neat and Graph draw a free map; in those, each account is a group that opens into tiles, capped at 24 with the rest behind a **+N** tile.
  - **Show what agents use**: whether the assets beneath agents are drawn.
  - **Expand every account** / **Collapse every account**.
- **Ask**: opens the assistant dock, Ctrl, to ask about the estate. Shown only when the assistant module is on and you hold `chat:ask`.

## The focus view

**One asset and what it touches** draws one asset as a tree. **Centre on** picks the asset, or **Whole estate**. An asset's **Open in Shadow AI map** action opens this view on that asset.

## The details panel

Selecting an account shows its id, the number of its assets by kind, and **Open in AI Inventory**.

Selecting an asset shows its registry badge with the next step (**Register it**, **Review in Approvals** or **Open in Registry**), then **Source**, **Type**, **Account**, **Region**, **Status**, its description, its **Connections** (each relationship, clickable), up to 14 **Properties**, and two links: **Focus view** and **Open asset**.

## Empty states

Before anything is discovered the map reads "Nothing discovered yet": connect an account and run a scan.
