The Overview is the console's home page. It sums up everything OpenController has discovered across your connected clouds and points you to the page where each number is worked on.

**Who can see it:** anyone with `assets:read`. A person without it is sent to the first page they can read instead: the Registry (`registry:read`), Observability (`observability:read`), or Settings.

## Actions in the header

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Estate report** | Builds a report of the estate in your browser, from what you are allowed to see: a PDF to read, or a workbook of its numbers to filter and chart. Building takes a few seconds. |
| **Run scan** | Starts a discovery scan of the connected clouds. |

## Shortcuts

A list of the console's pages, in the side panel's order, each with a one-line hint. Only pages whose module is on in your organization, and that you have permission for, are listed.

## The four numbers

| Number | What it counts | Where it comes from | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assets** | Every live asset in the inventory, with how many kinds | `GET /v1/assets/summary` | AI Inventory, all kinds |
| **Agents** | Assets of kind agent, with the number of MCP servers beneath | `GET /v1/assets/summary` | AI Inventory, agents |
| **Registered** | The share of governed assets that are registered. Governed means registered, awaiting approval or shadow. The line beneath reads "N of M governed · P awaiting approval" | `GET /v1/registry/summary` | Registry |
| **Shadow** | Discovered assets of a kind the registry policy names that nobody has registered | `GET /v1/registry/summary` | Shadow AI |

When the register module is off, **Registered** and **Shadow** are replaced by one number, **Integrations**: the connections that are enabled.

## Governance

Two panels, shown when the register module is on. Both color assets by where they stand in the registry: red for shadow, amber for awaiting approval, green for registered, grey for not governed.

- **Assets by status**: one row per asset kind, shadow included. Each row's bar is as long as the kind is big and split by registry standing. Clicking a segment opens the page that holds those assets.
- **Agent landscape**: every agent, grouped by **Source** (the product it was built in), **Cloud**, **Registry**, **State** or **Type**, biggest group first. Chips above the list narrow it to one state: **All**, **Active**, **Draft**, **Inactive** or **Not applicable**. The columns are the grouping, **Registry**, **Agents** and **Share**.

## Findings

Shown when the posture module is on. The list is the checks that have open findings, worst first, each with the number of assets it found. Until the first evaluation has run, the card says so. **All findings** opens the Findings page.

## Latest scan

Shown when the discover module is on. The card names the cloud scanned and when the scan started, then its status badge, how long it took, **Sources read** and **Sources failed**. **Scan details** opens the scan. Before any scan has run, the card reads "No scans yet" and tells you to connect an account and run a scan.

The shell polls for the newest scan, so this card stays current while a scan runs.

## API

| Method | Path | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/v1/assets/summary` | Live assets counted by kind |
| `GET` | `/v1/registry/summary` | Registered, pending and shadow counts, overall and by kind |
| `GET` | `/v1/connections` | The connections and whether each is enabled |
