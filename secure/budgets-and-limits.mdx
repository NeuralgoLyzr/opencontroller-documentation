<Note>Needs the **LLM Gateway** module. See [Modules](/administer/modules).</Note>

A team gathers the virtual keys of the agents one group runs. A customer gathers teams, for spend billed to one client. A budget or rate limit on a team or customer caps every key under it, together. A key can also carry its own limits: a call is refused as soon as it would go past the key's limit or its team's, whichever comes first. When you finish, you have a team (or customer) with a budget and rate limits, and keys counted against it.

<Info>
This team is the LLM gateway's, under **LLM Gateway** > **Teams & Customers**. It is not the same as the teams under **Settings** > **Teams**, which decide what people in OpenController may see.
</Info>

<Note>
**Before you start**
- You need the **Operator** role or higher to create teams and customers. A **Viewer** can read the list. Putting keys under a team needs an **Admin**.
- The LLM gateway is connected. See [Connection](/secure/gateway-overview#connection).
- The agents' virtual keys exist, one per agent. See [Give apps virtual keys](/secure/virtual-keys).
- **Customers** show only when the connected gateway supports them.
</Note>

## Create a team or customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Teams & Customers">
    Go to **LLM Gateway** > **Teams & Customers**. If the gateway supports customers, choose the **Teams** or **Customers** tab.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: teams-list | crop | LLM Gateway > Teams & Customers, Teams tab, with Support, Billing and Research: Customer column, Budget bars (Support at about 80%), Rate limit, and the New team button. Crop to the page content, no sidebar. */}
    <Frame caption="The Teams tab">
      <img src="/images/llm-gateway/teams-list.png" alt="The Teams list with each team's customer, budget use and rate limit." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select New team or New customer">
    The button at the top right follows the tab you are on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    - **Name**: for example `Support`. Up to 128 characters.
    - **Customer** (optional, teams only): the customer the team belongs to, or **No customer**.
    - Under **Limits · leave empty for none**:
      - **Budget**: dollars per period, for example `500`, **Monthly**.
      - **Tokens**: tokens per period, for example `1000000`, **Daily**.
      - **Requests**: requests per period, for example `1000`, **Hourly**.

      Leave a field empty for no limit. The periods offered (**Per minute**, **Hourly**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**) are the ones the connected gateway supports. A gateway with no rate limits shows only **Budget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create**. The team shows in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put keys under it">
    An **Admin** does this for each of the team's keys:

    1. Go to **LLM Gateway** > **Virtual Keys**.
    2. On the key, open its menu and select **Edit**.
    3. Set **Belongs to** to **Team · Support**. The key's spend now also counts against the team's budget.
    4. Select **Save**.

    See [Give apps virtual keys](/secure/virtual-keys).

    <Tip>Keep a smaller budget on each key as well, so one agent cannot spend the whole team's budget.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit or delete

Open the menu (**⋯**) at the end of the row:

- **Edit** opens the same form. Select **Save** when done.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm. The team's or customer's virtual keys stay, no longer grouped under it. Its budget is deleted with it.

## Check it worked

The row shows:
- **Budget**: the spend so far against the budget, for example `$0.00 of $500.00 a month`, with a bar. The bar turns amber at 70% and red at 90%.
- **Rate limit**: the limits, or **No rate limit**.
- **Customer** (teams, when the gateway has customers): the customer, or a dash.

As keys under the team make calls, the team's name shows next to the key under **Caller** in [Logs](/secure/logs).

## What happens at the limit

When a limit is reached, the gateway refuses the call until the period resets:

| Limit reached | The gateway answers |
|---|---|
| **Budget** of the key or the team | HTTP 402 |
| **Tokens** or **Requests** of the key or the team | HTTP 429 |

Your agents should handle both: tell the user, and retry a 429 later. Do not retry a 402 in a loop: nothing changes until the budget's period resets or someone raises it.

## Watch the spend

- **LLM Gateway** > **Teams & Customers**: the **Budget** column shows what each team has spent of its budget in this period.
- **LLM Gateway** > **Overview**: **Closest to budget** lists the keys nearest their own budget.
- **LLM Gateway** > **Logs**: the cost of every call, and the key and team that made it.
