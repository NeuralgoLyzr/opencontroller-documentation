- **Model** shows **via** and the rule's name when a [routing rule](/secure/routing-and-mcp#routing-rules) sent the call.
- **Caller** names the virtual key and its team or customer, not a person. To see who made a call, the key needs **Callers sign in with**. See [Virtual keys](/secure/virtual-keys).
- To see why a call failed, set **Status** to **Failed** and hover the **Failed** badge.
- Prompts and answers stay in the gateway: OpenController lists only the calls. **Search prompts and answers** shows only when the gateway supports it.

Every role, from **Viewer** up, can read the logs.
