## General: rename the organization

**General** is the organization itself. Every asset, integration and grant belongs to it. **Organization ID** is read only: it is the tenant key on every record, set where OpenController is deployed.

## Members: add, change and remove people

**Members** lists the people who sign in with an email and password. People who sign in through single sign-on are not listed: they get their role from your identity provider. See [Single sign-on](/administer/sso).

There is no self sign-up and no email invite. An admin adds each person with an **Initial password** of at least 8 characters, and gives it to them over a channel you trust. They can change it after they sign in.

<Warning>The password is shown once. After you select **Done**, you cannot see it again. To replace it, use **Reset password**.</Warning>

<Frame caption="Settings > Members">
  <img src="/images/settings/org-member-actions.png" alt="The Members table with each person's role, status and last sign-in, and the Add user button." />

From a row's **⋯** menu:
- **Role**: the change applies at once.
- **Reset password**: their current password and sessions stop working at once.
- **Disable**: they can no longer sign in, and their sessions end. Use it instead of **Remove** to stop someone for a while.
- **Remove**: their sessions end. What they did stays in the [audit log](/administer/audit-log).

Rules that always apply:
- Nobody can change their own role, disable themselves or remove themselves.
- Only an **Owner** can change an **Owner** or make someone an **Owner**.
- There is always at least one active **Owner**. The last one cannot be removed, disabled or given a lower role.

## Roles: see and make roles

A role is a set of permissions, one for each thing OpenController does. The API checks a permission on every request. Who gets which role is set in **Members** and [Role mappings](/administer/sso#map-groups-to-roles).

{/* SCREENSHOT: org-roles | crop | Settings > Roles: the four System roles (Owner, Admin, Operator, Viewer) and one Custom role "auditor", with the Type and Permissions columns visible. Crop to the section header and table. */}
<Frame caption="The Roles table">
  <img src="/images/settings/org-roles.png" alt="The Roles table with four System roles and one Custom role, each with its permission count." />
</Frame>

Each built-in role holds everything the role below it holds, and more:

| Role | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Read everything, ask Ctrl |
| **Operator** | Also scan, verify and change agent sharing |
| **Admin** | Also manage integrations and users |
| **Owner** | Also the organization itself: single sign-on, access rules, owners |

See [Roles and permissions](/get-started/roles) for the full list.

### Make a custom role

A custom role holds exactly the permissions you give it. You can map it from identity provider groups, give it to a member, or give it to a service account. Only an **Owner** can select **Add role**.

- **Name**: 2 to 32 lowercase letters, digits and dashes, and not a built-in role's name.
- Every role can always see and change itself.
- When you remove a custom role, people who held it, or were mapped to it, keep only the default role, if one is set.

<Info>If a person matches a built-in role and a custom role, the built-in role wins. Among custom roles, the one with the most permissions wins.</Info>

## Teams: group people and the assets they own

A team links your identity provider's groups to the assets the team owns. Everyone in a linked group is in the team, with the role you give that group. A role in a team adds to a person's role in the organization. It never takes anything away.

- **Groups**: each linked group gets **Viewer**, **Operator** or **Admin** in the team. A person in several groups gets the highest role. Microsoft Entra ID sends groups by id, so pick them from the provisioned list or type the id.
- **Also people added by hand**: adds local accounts, or someone not yet in a group.
- **Assets**: the accounts or environments the team owns. You can also give a single asset to a team from its own page.
- **LLM gateway team** (optional): the id of the gateway's team whose virtual keys and budget belong to this team.

When you delete a team, its members lose what the team gave them, and what it owned is owned by no team, unless another team's rules cover it.

## Who can use it

- Every role reads **General**. Only an **Admin** or **Owner** can change it.
- **Admin** manages members and teams.
- **Owner** also adds and changes roles, and changes or makes **Owners**.
