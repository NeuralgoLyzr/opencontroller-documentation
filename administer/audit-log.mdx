**AI assets** records actions taken on agents and other assets from the console. **Identity & access** records sign-ins, and changes to people, service accounts, keys, roles, teams, single sign-on and settings. The log refreshes every 15 seconds.

## What it records, and what it does not

- Events about credentials (connections, keys, guardrail connections) name the fields that changed, never their values.
- Guardrail and OPA policy events name the guardrail or policy, never the text tried on it or its Rego.
- The text of prompts and answers is never recorded.
- Changes to secrets are in **Settings** > **Secrets** > **Activity**. See [Secrets](/administer/secrets-auth#activity).

**Identity & access** shows up to the latest 200 events.

<Tip>To see one actor's events, or more than 200, call the API: `GET /v1/audit/events?actor=alice@example.com&limit=500`. It takes `kind`, `actor` and `limit` (up to 500).</Tip>

## Who can use it

- **Viewer** and **Operator** see only the **AI assets** list, without tabs.
- **Admin** and **Owner** also see **Identity & access** (the `audit:read` permission). A custom role can hold `audit:read` too, for example an `auditor` role. See [Make a custom role](/administer/members-roles-teams#make-a-custom-role).
