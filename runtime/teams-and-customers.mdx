A team gathers the virtual keys of the agents one group runs. A customer gathers teams and keys whose spend is
billed to one client. Both exist for two reasons: attribution, so logs and spend show which group a call belongs to,
and budgets, so one limit caps every key under the group together.

Customers appear only when the connected gateway has them. Rekori does.

## The list

**LLM Gateway**, then **Teams & customers**. The **Teams** and **Customers** tabs switch between the two; search
with **Search teams** or **Search customers**.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The team's or customer's name. |
| **Customer** | Teams only: the customer the team belongs to, if any. |
| **Budget** | Spend so far against the group's budget, with a bar. **No budget** when none is set. |
| **Rate limit** | The group's token and request limits. **No rate limit** when none is set. |

The actions menu on a row offers **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Create a team or customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On the **Teams** tab click **New team**; on the **Customers** tab click **New customer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    **Name** is required, up to 128 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="For a team, choose its customer">
    **Customer** is optional: **No customer**, or one of the customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set limits">
    Under **Limits**, leave a field empty for no limit. **Budget** is an amount in US dollars per period: hourly,
    daily, weekly or monthly. **Tokens** and **Requests** are rate limits per minute, hour, day, week or month. The
    budget caps the spend of every key under the group, together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link keys to a group

A key joins a team or a customer through its own form: on **Virtual keys**, edit the key and set **Belongs to**. A
key belongs to one team or one customer, not both. Its own budget still applies, and its spend also counts against
the group's.

Groups also appear where calls are attributed and rules are scoped:

- **Logs** shows the key and its team or customer in the **Caller** column.
- A content rule under **Content Rules** can apply to **Every request**, one team, or one key.

## Edit and delete

**Edit** opens the same form; a limit left empty is cleared. **Delete** asks you to confirm: the group's virtual keys
stay, no longer grouped under it, and its budget goes with it.

## API

- `GET /v1/llm-gateway/teams`, `POST /v1/llm-gateway/teams`: list and create teams.
- `PUT /v1/llm-gateway/teams/{id}`, `DELETE /v1/llm-gateway/teams/{id}`: change and delete a team.
- `GET /v1/llm-gateway/customers`, `POST /v1/llm-gateway/customers`: list and create customers.
- `PUT /v1/llm-gateway/customers/{id}`, `DELETE /v1/llm-gateway/customers/{id}`: change and delete a customer.

Lists take `search`, `limit` (1 to 200, default 50) and `offset`.
