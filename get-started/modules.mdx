The sidebar follows the flow: discover, govern, run, watch, improve. Sections appear only when the installation's
profile includes them and your role can read them.

## Overview and Spend

**Overview** is the estate on one page: how many agents, on which platforms, how many are registered, open findings
and approvals, and recent activity. **Spend** totals what the agents cost, across the gateway, observability and the
platforms, by provider, model, team and agent.

## Discover

**AI Inventory** is every asset discovery found, as a list and as a map. **People** is everyone the platforms name
as a creator or owner. **Shadow AI** is the unregistered part of the inventory, with the controls to register it.

## Govern

**Approvals** is the queue of registration requests. **Registry** is the list of approved agents, their owners and
their states. **Policies** is the registration policy: which kinds must be registered, what a request states and what an
approval needs. Deciding and governing need the Admin role.

## Control

Actions on an agent in its own platform: block, unblock, share, revoke, end organisation-wide sharing, delete. They
live on the agent's page in the inventory, and every one is audited.

<Card title="Control actions" href="/govern/control-actions" />

## Runtime

**LLM Gateway** is the endpoint in front of the model providers, with virtual keys, teams, logs and spend caps.
**Guardrails** are the checks that run on prompts and answers passing through it. **Guardrail Violations** is what
the checks caught.

## Integrations and Settings

**Integrations** is where connections are added, tested and rescanned. **Settings** holds the organisation (general, members, roles,
teams), the scan schedule, security (single sign-on, role mappings, service accounts, secrets, authentication, the
audit log) and each module's connection.

<Card title="Connections" href="/connect/overview" />

## Who sees what

| Role | Sees | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Viewer | Every section the profile includes | Read the inventory, registry, findings, scans and audit log; ask Ctrl |
| Operator | The same | Also run scans, request registrations, try guardrails, change agent sharing |
| Admin | The same, plus Policies and Guardrail Violations | Also decide approvals, change the registry policy, make guardrails, manage connections and credentials, pin owners and teams |
| Owner | Everything, including the Security settings | Also single sign-on, the access rules, and who holds which role |
