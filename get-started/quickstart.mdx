This walk-through assumes OpenController is installed and you have the administrator account created at
installation, or an Admin role from your administrator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the console. Sign in with your email and password, or with your identity provider if single sign-on is
    set up. If the console says **No access yet**, ask your administrator for the viewer, operator or admin role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect one platform">
    Open **Integrations** and add a connection for the platform with the most agents, usually Microsoft or AWS.
    Each platform's guide lists exactly what to create on the provider's side and which fields to fill in. A read
    credential is enough for this walk-through.

          <div className="oc-cards oc-cards-logos">
        <a className="oc-card" href="/connect/aws"><div className="oc-card-media is-logo"><CloudLogo name="aws" /></div><div className="oc-t">AWS</div></a>
        <a className="oc-card" href="/connect/microsoft"><div className="oc-card-media is-logo"><CloudLogo name="microsoft" /></div><div className="oc-t">Microsoft</div></a>
        <a className="oc-card" href="/connect/google-cloud"><div className="oc-card-media is-logo"><CloudLogo name="gcp" /></div><div className="oc-t">Google Cloud</div></a>
        <a className="oc-card" href="/connect/databricks"><div className="oc-card-media is-logo"><CloudLogo name="databricks" /></div><div className="oc-t">Databricks</div></a>
        <a className="oc-card" href="/connect/anthropic"><div className="oc-card-media is-logo"><CloudLogo name="anthropic" /></div><div className="oc-t">Anthropic</div></a>
        <a className="oc-card" href="/connect/snowflake"><div className="oc-card-media is-logo"><CloudLogo name="snowflake" /></div><div className="oc-t">Snowflake</div></a>
      </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan it">
    A new connection scans on its own. Open the connection to watch the scan, and read its result: how many assets
    it found and anything it could not read, which usually means a permission is missing.

    <Card icon="refresh-cw" title="Scans" href="/connect/scans" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the inventory">
    Open **AI Inventory**. The **Agents** tab lists every agent the scan found; **All assets** lists what they use.
    Filter by **Cloud** and **Source** to see one platform at a time, and open an agent to see its account, identity,
    models, tools and servers.

    <Card icon="boxes" title="AI Inventory" href="/discover/inventory" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find what nobody owns">
    Open **Shadow AI**. The map runs from accounts to products to agents to what they use, and the numbers at the top
    say how much of the estate is registered. Everything here is unregistered.

    <Card icon="scan-eye" title="Shadow AI" href="/discover/shadow-ai" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register one agent">
    Pick an agent you know, select it and click **Register selected**. Name its owner; discovery prefills the person
    the platform records. The request appears in **Approvals**, where an Admin registers or rejects it. Once
    registered it leaves Shadow AI and appears in the **Registry**.

    <Card icon="stamp" title="Approvals" href="/govern/approvals" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
