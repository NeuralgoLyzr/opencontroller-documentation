OpenController is read-only until you decide otherwise, so the rollout can move at the pace of your review, not
your change board. This is a plan that has the inventory in a week and the controls in a quarter.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Week one: see everything">
    Connect every platform with a read credential only. Start with the one that holds the most agents, usually
    Microsoft or AWS, and add the rest the same week. Let the first scans run. Open **Shadow AI** and read the four
    numbers across the top: that is your baseline, and the number you will report against.

    What you have at the end of the week: the inventory, the owners the platforms name, and the first findings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Month one: decide what counts, and triage">
    Set the registration policy: which asset kinds must be registered, what a request must state, and whether
    approvals need separation of duties. Work the findings from Critical down using the response framework. Register
    the agents you know in bulk, by connection, and let the owners confirm them through approvals.

    What you have at the end of the month: a registry with owners, a shrinking shadow count, and the critical
    findings closed or accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quarter one: take control and watch">
    Grant a control credential on the platforms where you need to act, and use it: narrow sharing, block what should
    not run, delete what is abandoned. Put new applications behind the LLM gateway with guardrails and a budget per
    team. Send the agents that matter most to observability and set up evaluators for them.

    What you have at the end of the quarter: a stop button, guardrails on the traffic you choose, traces for the
    agents that matter, and a framework mapping you can hand to an auditor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to report

- **Coverage:** connected platforms, and the share of governed assets that are registered.
- **Exposure:** open findings by severity, and the time to close Critical ones.
- **Ownership:** agents with a named owner, and agents whose owner has left.
- **Runtime:** share of model traffic through the gateway, guardrail trips, spend by team.

## What not to do

- Do not start with control credentials. Discovery needs none, and the inventory is the argument for everything
  after it.
- Do not try to register everything by hand. Register by connection, and let approvals and owners sort it.
- Do not block before registering. A block applies only to a registered or pending agent, and the Block dialog can
  register in the same step, so that the audit trail starts at the first action.
