A finding is a posture problem on one asset, raised by one of the [security policies](/posture/security-policies). Findings are evaluated again after each scan, and when someone selects **Evaluate now**.

<Info>**Governance** findings flag assets nobody registered or owns: register them in the [Registry](/govern/registry). **Runtime protection** findings flag agents whose calls skip the LLM gateway and guardrails: give them a [virtual key](/secure/virtual-keys) on the gateway.</Info>

## Open a policy and fix it

**Policies** lists each turned-on policy with something open, worst first. Select one to see **How to fix it**, the assets it is open on with the evidence, and the framework items it answers to.

{/* SCREENSHOT: finding-detail | crop | The panel for policy DAT-1 "Credentials in environment variables": How to fix it, Open on N assets, Frameworks it answers to. Crop to the side panel. */}
<img className="w-[480px] mx-auto rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700" src="/images/posture/finding-detail.png" alt="A policy's side panel with how to fix it, the assets it is open on, and the frameworks it answers to." />

A finding closes when the problem is fixed on the asset: at the next evaluation it moves to **Resolved in the last 30 days**. To stop a policy for every asset, turn it off on [Security policies](/posture/security-policies).

If a policy could not run, for example because an integration it reads is off, it says **Not assessed at the last evaluation**.

### Why a finding's severity differs from its policy's

A finding can be more severe than its policy. Each of these on the asset raises it one step: **Reachable from outside**, **Can act** and **Reaches data** (up to **Critical**), and **Heavily used** (up to **High**). **Raised by** on the finding names which. A factor that is the policy's own condition does not raise it again.

## Who can use it

- **Viewer** reads and exports findings. A role held through a team shows only that team's assets' findings.
- **Operator** can also select **Evaluate now**.
- **Admin** and **Owner** can also turn policies on and off.
